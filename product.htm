THE PRODUCT / 01

A bolted
panel system.

Water storage tanks are built from different materials, including concrete, metal and plastic. Stainless steel's strength, flexibility, ease of cleaning and corrosion resistance make it suitable for water containment.

MY TANK's modular panels are available in 1 m × 1 m and 1.22 m × 1.22 m formats. Panels can be transported in bundles, bolted together on site and individually replaced if needed.

PANEL SYSTEM / 02

Pressed.
Sealed. Bolted.

A nontoxic sealant between panels creates a watertight connection. Bolted assembly uses an impact wrench. Individual panels can be changed during repair.

THE PRACTICAL CASE / 04

Built for access,
transport & service.

Transportable

Up to 25 panels fit in a pallet bundle. This equates to one 24,000 litre tank, and each pallet weighs less than 1000 kg.

Adaptable

The modular format allows different tank sizes, shapes and locations during installation and if needs change later.

Serviceable

If repair is required, individual affected panels can be replaced. With non-modular tanks, damage can mean replacing the entire tank.
