01 / THE SYSTEM

A tank
built from
parts.

MY TANK water tanks are modular, using stainless steel panels that can be transported and assembled on site.

02 / THE ASSEMBLY

Every panel
has a place.

Panels are bolted together with a nontoxic sealant between them. Assembly uses an impact wrench and needs no special skills or tools.

03 / THE RESULT

Replace a panel.
Resize the tank.

The tank's size, shape and location can adapt to changing needs. If repair is required, the affected panels can be replaced individually.

MANUFACTURING / 03

From plate to
hydrostatic test.

A six-stage process takes each panel from quality-controlled plate through hydrostatic testing.

PROCESS / MANUFACTURING
01

Raw material

Each stainless steel plate is checked for exact thickness and covered with a protective film against scratches and foreign particles.

02

Fiber laser cutting

Panels are cut with a fiber laser for precise, burr-free edges without further grinding.

03

High-power press forming

Clean molds and a 1000MT to 1500MT press are used to form the panels.

04

Robotic corner welding

Panels are welded at each corner to form flanges. Robotic welding supports consistent quality.

05

Pickling & passivation

Pickling and passivation follow ASTM A380 Standard Practice for Cleaning, Descaling and Passivation of Stainless Steel Parts.

06

Hydrostatic testing

Hydrostatic testing completes the manufacturing sequence.

THE MODULAR ADVANTAGE / 05

A tank, delivered
as panels.

01 / TRANSPORT

From plant to site.

Panels are transported in pallets of up to 25 per bundle, equating to one 24,000 litre tank. Each pallet weighs less than 1000 kg and can be unloaded separately.

02 / INSTALLATION

Assembly with less equipment.

Individual pallets can be transported to rural areas. Each pallet can be unloaded separately without cranes or other heavy equipment. The bolting system requires only an impact wrench.

03 / SERVICE

Replace the part, not the whole.

If a tank needs repair, the affected panels can be changed individually. The modular design also allows later changes to size, shape or location.
